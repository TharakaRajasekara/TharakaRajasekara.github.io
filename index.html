The program

Movement that lifts your mood

Zumba for Mental Wellness brings fun, music-driven movement into University of Peradeniya hostels. Sessions are run with the Counselling and Psychological Support Unit (CaPSU) to give students a safe, social way to take a break from academic pressure.

Dance it out

Easy-to-follow Latin and world rhythms. No experience needed, no judgement, just move.

SalsaBachataCumbiaCha-chaMerengue

Calm your mind

Every session ends with a slow cool-down and breathing, so you leave relaxed, not just tired.

Connect with friends

Meet people from your hostel and build a healthy weekly habit together.

Meet your instructor

Ms. Tharaka Rajasekara

I lead Zumba® for Mental Wellness · Sri Lanka, an independent wellness initiative. I started free hostel sessions at the University of Peradeniya because I saw how much academic pressure students carry, and how much lighter people feel after an hour of music and movement together.

Licensed Zumba® InstructorZumba Instructor, CaPSUResearcher, University of PeradeniyaFaculty of Engineering

Alongside teaching, I research how dance-based movement relates to students' stress, anxiety and mood, so every session is built on care and evidence.
